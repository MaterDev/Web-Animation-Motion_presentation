- **Roost — the proof**
  - **131,072** starlings
  - Each reacts to its six or seven nearest neighbours, after Ballerini et al. 2008 **[29]**
  - Every bird reads its neighbours directly in GPU memory
- **Range**
  - **Vermeer**, *Young Woman with a Water Pitcher*, through a refracting loupe — WebGL, CC0 from The Met
  - **Marble bust** — WebGL, photogrammetry scan, CC0 from Poly Haven
  - **Supercell** — a storm that runs itself, on WebGPU
- **Why the bust and Vermeer are WebGL**
  - WebGL's limit isn't motion; it's lots of **interacting** state
