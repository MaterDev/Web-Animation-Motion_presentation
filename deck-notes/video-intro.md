- **Good at**
  - Cheapest way to put motion on a screen
  - The only pre-baked format you can tell to stop
- **Costs: it's fixed**
  - ***Video can't respond*** — not to a cursor, scroll or click
  - Once motion has to respond, video isn't cheaper; it isn't an option
- **Three uses we haven't tried, and could**
  1. **Filters on captured video** — effects on a live camera feed, Instagram-style
  2. **Computer vision** on video or live streams — adjacent, not shown
  3. **Video as a component** — keyed footage layered so it reads as part of the page
- ***The video is not the content, it's a material***
