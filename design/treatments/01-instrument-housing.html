surface & material

03

The point where this either lands or becomes generic glassmorphism. Both panels below sit over the same "internals" — the difference is entirely in the treatment.

tinted polycarbonate
coloured transmission · uneven density · hard specular edge
✓Tint is saturated and coloured, so it colours what's behind rather than fogging it. Density varies across the panel. Sharp top-edge highlight, not a soft 1px border. You can see real internals.
generic glassmorphism
uniform white blur · even border · reads as 2020
✕Uniform blur with a white overlay. Fogs rather than tints, density is flat, the border is even all the way round. Could be any product from the last five years.
The internals should be honest. First preference is the live demo itself, dimmed behind the panel. Second is a PCB trace pattern generated from the actual slide graph — nodes are slides, traces are navigation paths. That makes the deck's own structure the circuit board, which is on-thesis.

Duration scale

TokenValueApplied to
--dur-instant90msFocus, press feedback
--dur-fast160msHover, toggle, small state change
--dur-base240msSlide advance — happens 200+ times before delivery. Must feel immediate, not like an effect.
--dur-slow420msPanel, overlay, overview grid
--dur-section700msSection tint change — experiential, happens 6×
--dur-hero1400msCold open, WebGPU reveal — once each
Frequency is inversely proportional to intensity. The whole duration scale is that one rule. Slide advance is the highest-frequency action in the app, so it gets the most restraint; the cold open happens once and can be lavish.
Reduced motion substitutes, it doesn't delete. Transitions become pure cross-fades — travel removed, the fact of the change preserved. Toggle it in your OS to see this sheet respond. A visible chrome indicator when it's active turns an accessibility feature into a live demo asset for slide 13.
