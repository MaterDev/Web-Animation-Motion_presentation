what changed

00

Four things are structurally different from T-01, not just re-valued. They're flagged in place below.

1 · Elevation moves from lightness to shadow. On dark, cast shadow reads as smudge and surfaces had to rise by getting lighter. On light, shadow is physically correct — so --lift-1/2/3 exist here and have no counterpart in T-01.
2 · Internals invert to a cutaway. A lit field on a light ground is invisible. The aperture is now a dark recess showing a real board, which is closer to how all four reference products actually photograph: pale housing, dark window.
3 · The readout becomes an LCD, not an LED. A glowing dot-matrix display is meaningless on a light chassis. It becomes a recessed monochrome LCD — dark figures on a green-grey field. Game Boy DMG and the OP-1, rather than Nothing's Glyph.
4 · Tints drop in lightness and gain chroma. T-01's tints wash out completely here. Same hues, ~0.15 lower lightness, higher chroma.

Housing — light ramp

Base is grey, not white. White is reserved for raised surfaces, so panels read as sitting above the chassis rather than being cut out of it. Note the ramp is deliberately not monotonic — 000→200 climb toward white as surfaces rise, then 300→900 descend for rules and text.

Text floor is still 500 — but it's a different risk here. On dark, thin light text on black smears under projection. On light, the failure is the opposite: projectors wash out light backgrounds and lift blacks, so mid-greys lose contrast against the ground. Light is generally the safer projection choice in a room with ambient light, which is the strongest practical argument for this treatment.

The LCD carries its own dead segments. The faint 88:88 ghosted behind the live figures is what an unlit segment actually looks like on a real display. It's the single detail that stops this reading as "dark text in a green box" — and it costs one absolutely-positioned element.
Legibility floor is a room, not a viewport. Body copy on a slide starts at ~24px effective, headlines 64px+. On light, also watch weight: Inter 200 at display size is fine, but don't let it below ~300 for anything smaller.

surface & material

03

Both panels sit over the same dark aperture. On a light chassis this comparison is more decisive than on dark — white-on-dark glassmorphism is at least plausible, whereas white-on-light simply disappears.

tinted polycarbonate
coloured transmission · uneven density · hard specular edge
✓Tint density raised to ~40% — on light the panel has to hold its colour against a dark aperture. Reads as moulded plastic over a board.
generic glassmorphism
uniform white blur · even border · reads as 2020
✕A white veil over a dark hole. No material identity at all, and the edge treatment does nothing. Worse here than in T-01.
The internals should be honest. First preference is the live demo itself behind the panel. Second is a PCB trace pattern generated from the actual slide graph — nodes are slides, traces are navigation paths.

One motion consequence of going light. A full-screen cross-fade between light slides is a large bright area changing — less likely to read as flicker than on dark, but far more fatiguing over 20 minutes if brightness swings between slides. Keep slide grounds at a consistent lightness; vary tint, not luminance.
