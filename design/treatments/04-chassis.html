what changed

T-03 → T-04

Nothing in the colour system moved. Every change here is material — the sheet stops describing a device and becomes one.

Panels are manufactured parts. Every surface is a moulding with a crisp light-catch on its top edge and shadow beneath — bolted down with hex screws, vented, and split by parting lines where two mouldings meet.
Callouts replace captions. Numbered flags on leader lines with a key underneath. This is the single most recognisable move in a TE manual and it turns a layout into documentation.
Real controls. A rotary encoder, a toggle, LED indicators. These aren't decoration — the deck genuinely has a scrub control, a reduced-motion toggle and status states, so the physical metaphor maps to real function.
Display type doubled. Headlines to ~130px, tracking to -0.05em. TE sets product names enormous and lowercase; at T-03's size the type was polite rather than confident.
The risk is decoration. Screws and vents are honest on a hero panel and become noise if applied to every box. They appear on the front panel, the exploded view and the control module only.
01

assembly

Fig. 1

The app's four layers as an exploded diagram. This is the architecture from DESIGN.md drawn the way a kit manual would draw it — and it doubles as a real explanation of how a slide renders.

FIG. 1 — ASSEMBLY ORDER
4Chrome / UI 3Runtime 2Deck data 1Chassis / Stage
  • 1Chassis / Stage — the fixed 16:9 surface everything mounts to. Never re-renders.
  • 2Deck data — plain serialisable slides. No JSX, no imports. Swappable without touching code.
  • 3Runtime — navigation, steps, timing, sync, demo lifecycle. Knows nothing about visual design.
  • 4Chrome / UI — the only layer that carries this treatment. Swap it and the deck still runs.
The diagram is the argument for the architecture. Layers 1-3 are identical across every treatment on this sheet; only layer 4 changes. That's why T-01 through T-04 share component CSS and differ by tokens alone.
