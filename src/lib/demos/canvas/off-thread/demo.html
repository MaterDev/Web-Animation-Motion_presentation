Both players are drawn, and drawn the same way: a blitter. Flat colours, integer rectangles, a hand-set 5×6 bitmap font, and bevels that are four colours and six lines with no interpolation — a bevel here is a notation for depth, the way a contour line is, not a simulation of light. The font is hand-set rather than rasterised because text rendering is the least portable thing in a browser and the parity check below is byte-exact; a bitmap font cannot disagree with itself, which is also why skins shipped one. The scrolling title is the mark that freezes — a stalled marquee is exactly what a hung player looked like, and everyone in the room has seen one. Both strings are duplicated in the DOM, because a raster is somewhere a screen reader cannot go.
